## 🧠 the slightly chaotic career plan

```text
                    ┌──────────────────────┐
                    │       COMPUTER       │
                    │        SCIENCE       │
                    └──────────┬───────────┘
                               │
               ┌───────────────┼───────────────┐
               ↓               ↓               ↓
           🎨 DESIGN        🧠 AI/LLMs      🔬 RESEARCH
               │               │               │
               └───────────────┼───────────────┘
                               ↓
                    ✨ BUILD COOL THINGS ✨
```

I want to sit somewhere in the middle of:

**beautiful interfaces × intelligent systems × weird ideas**

Long-term, I'd love to explore **UI/UX design**, creative technology, and the science behind **LLMs and intelligent systems**.

Basically:

> make it beautiful
> make it useful
> then open the hood and figure out *why it works*
